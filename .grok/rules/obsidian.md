# Obsidian

Vault: AI Wiki. Before any read or write, open the vault-root **`Grok.md`** and follow it.

Short version (the file wins if this disagrees):

- Folders are note *type*. `1 - Rough Notes` is inbox. `6 - Wiki` is atomic claims. `2 - Source Material` is sources. `4 - Indexes` is maps.
- New problem/solution from this repo: a dated note in `1 - Rough Notes`. Do not turn the inbox into a second wiki.
- Front matter: `type`, `status` (`baby` on create), `created`, `updated`, 1–3 durable tags.
- Titles are claims. Own words. `[[Note Name]]` wikilinks, 1–4 of them.
- Never modify `.raw/`. Do not invent `wiki/concepts/` trees. Do not mention agent tooling in notes.
- Query `6 - Wiki` and `4 - Indexes` first.
